# Code of conduct

This project is made by people who like the same old racing game. Keep it a place where anyone can take part.

**Be respectful.** Treat everyone the way you would like to be treated, whatever their experience, background or
identity. Disagree with ideas, not with people, and assume good intent until shown otherwise.

**No harassment.** No insults, slurs, personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, or sharing someone's
private information. Don't keep pushing someone who has asked you to stop.

**Keep discussion on the work.** Issues, pull requests and discussions are for the port, its mods and its
documentation. Stay on topic, keep criticism constructive and specific, and don't ask for or share copies of the game
or its files.

**Moderation.** Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, lock threads, close issues and pull requests, and
block people from the project when this code is not followed. Usually a reminder comes first; serious or repeated
cases may skip it.

**Reporting.** If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact a maintainer privately (through the contact on the
maintainers' GitHub profiles) rather than replying in public. Reports are handled discreetly.

This code applies in all of the project's spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere someone represents the project.
