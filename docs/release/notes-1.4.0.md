# Burnout 3 native 1.4.0 (release notes)

A new EXTRAS page full of new ways to play: Eliminator in Single Event, race options, up to eight cars, the
Heavyweights, every traffic vehicle and your own car mods on SELECT CAR, traffic checking, party modes and race replays,
plus night and rain to try out. Your own colours on SELECT CAR, a camera you can turn in a race, reflections that show
the world around the car, Direct3D 12 on Windows, cleaner sound, smoother motion, a lighter load on the processor,
and fixes for stutter in the menus and crashes in heavy traffic.

## New

- **SETTINGS > EXTRAS.** Everything this update adds to the game itself has its own On / Off on one page, and **all of
  it is off on a new install except ELIMINATOR**, so the game plays as it shipped until you turn something on. The
  sections: SINGLE EVENT, SELECT OPTIONS, SELECT CAR, GAMEPLAY, PARTY MODES, EXPERIMENTAL and REPLAYS. A race with a
  gameplay extra keeps its results apart: no game records, nothing added to your driver's totals and unlocks, and its
  ghosts and best times in a folder of their own. A replay plays with the extras it was recorded with.
- **Eliminator in Single Event** (on by default). SELECT MODE has ELIMINATOR: World Tour's Eliminator on any race
  track, with the game's own rules and results page. **TIMED ELIMINATION** (optional) puts the last car out every 24
  seconds instead of at each lap, with a countdown on screen. Eliminator races are kept out of your progress.
- **Race options in Single Event.** Turn on any of these rows in EXTRAS > SELECT OPTIONS and SELECT OPTIONS shows it
  under REGION, TRACK and RIVALS (on two pages when they don't all fit: the last row, MORE, switches page). At their
  defaults, the race is the game's own:
  - **TRAFFIC**: Off, Light, Normal, Busy, Heavy or Extreme. **TRAFFIC PATTERN**: Fixed (the game's) or Varied.
  - **LAPS** (Race): 1 to 8. **RIVALS** (Road Rage): 1 to 5.
  - **RIVALS' CARS** (Race, Road Rage): your class, one series for all of them (Heavyweights included), Same as yours,
    or **Mixed** (each rival from a series of its own you have unlocked).
  - **START**: Rolling or **Standing**, with the game's own BOOST START.
  - **RIVAL STYLE** (Race): the rivals drive as in Road Rage.
  - **TIME LIMIT** (Road Rage): 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes, with the time left on the HUD.
  - **BURNOUT POINTS** (Road Rage): boost from driving, not only from takedowns.
  - **TRANSMISSION**: Manual, the game's own manual gearbox. SHIFT UP (U) and SHIFT DOWN (Y), or a controller's
    paddles; the gear shows above the speedometer.
  - **TRAFFIC CHECK**: see Traffic checking below.
- **Up to eight cars.** With MORE RIVALS on, RACE's RIVALS goes up to 7.
- **More cars on SELECT CAR** (EXTRAS > SELECT CAR; each series keeps its races' results apart):
  - **HEAVYWEIGHTS** in Race, Time Attack, Road Rage and Eliminator, once you have unlocked one: the buses, trucks
    and pick-ups from Crash mode, racing heavyweight rivals.
  - **TRAFFIC** and **TRAFFIC SPECIAL**: every traffic vehicle you meet on the road, from cars, taxis and the tuk tuk
    to buses and big rigs. Each drives like a race car of its kind and size.
  - **CUSTOM**: your car mods as cars of their own beside the game's, never in place of one. The launcher's Game Data
    page imports a car mod or a whole car pack (up to 200 cars, 19 a page).
  - **BETA**: the cars cut from the game, imported by the launcher from your own disc image of the June 2004 PS2
    prototype or demo, converted on your computer.
  - The launcher's Showroom groups its cars by series, with the traffic vehicles and your own cars.
- **The car viewer** (EXTRAS > SELECT CAR > CAR VIEWER): the game's own car viewer, which its menus never open. In
  YOUR GARAGE, press Select (Tab) to look at the car on show on its own: open its doors, crumple it, switch its lights.
- **Traffic checking** (TRAFFIC CHECK on SELECT OPTIONS; TRAFFIC CHECK (2 PLAYERS) in EXTRAS > GAMEPLAY).
  **Revenge**: ram a car, van or lorry going your way at 75 mph or more and it is launched ahead, as in Burnout Revenge,
  with chains, TRICK SHOT, SKILL SHOT and EXTREME SHOT. **Extreme**: any traffic you hit hard enough to crash you is
  shoved aside. The car you checked, and anything it wrecks, never crashes you.
- **More ways to play** (EXTRAS > GAMEPLAY):
  - **MIRROR MODE**: Single Event and two-player races on mirrored tracks.
  - **CRASHBREAKER IN EVERY MODE**: blow up your wreck after a crash in Race, Time Attack and Road Rage. CRASHBREAKER
    AFTER sets how many vehicles your crash must hit first; **Full** wrecks the rivals in the blast too.
  - **DEFORMATION**: how much the cars crumple, from None to 200%.
  - **ROAD RAGE PARTS: All loose**: in a single-event Road Rage every loose part can come off a damaged car.
  - **NO IDLE PAUSE** and **NO EVENT TIME LIMIT** turn off the game's pause after 5 minutes without input and the end
    of an event still running after 25 to 40 minutes.
- **PARTY MODES** (for fun): crazy AI physics (up to Mayhem), moon jump, crash on demand, an AI driver button,
  kamikaze traffic, each on a button you can set, and **TUK-TUKS**: you and every rival in the tuk tuk from the city
  traffic.
- **Watch a race again** (EXTRAS > REPLAYS > RACE REPLAY). A race's results page offers **Replay** (F6, or Square / X):
  the game's own replay of the race you have just driven, in every offline mode that has one. While it plays, pick the
  camera (Chase, Far chase, Bumper, Orbit, TV), the car it follows, or photo mode's free camera.
- **Night and rain, experimental** (EXTRAS > EXPERIMENTAL). **NIGHT** gives SINGLE EVENT's SELECT OPTIONS a **TIME OF
  DAY** row (Day / Night) and **RAIN** a **WEATHER** row (Clear / Rain), together a race in the rain at night; **NIGHT /
  RAIN IN WORLD TOUR** (Always or Random) take World Tour's events into the dark or the rain. At night the street lamps, signs, shop windows, neon and tunnels are lit,
  and the cars' tail lights glow. Rain brings falling rain, wet roads and puddles that mirror the lights, wet car paint,
  spray from the wheels, drops on the lens and the sound of the rain, quieter under bridges and in tunnels. Both only
  change what you see and hear: your progress and records count as usual. They are still being finished and are in
  this version for your feedback: please tell us what you think.
- **Paint on SELECT CAR.** Press **Paint** (Circle / Y, or P) on SELECT CAR to open a panel. It colours the car's
  body, decals, wheels and boost glow, with a colour picker, and FACTORY COLOURS puts the game's colours back. The
  small cars in the carousel show your colours. A new **Chrome** finish joins Gloss, Metallic and Pearl. The picture
  only: the game, replays and saves keep the paint you picked.
- **Look around in a race.** The right stick, or the mouse with its right button held, turns the chase camera around
  your car and eases back when you let go. It stops short of walls. Not on the PlayStation layout, where the right
  stick is the throttle; the mouse still works there. DISPLAY > CAMERA > ORBIT CAMERA turns it off.
- **More camera settings** (DISPLAY > CAMERA; the picture only):
  - **CAMERA ROLL** makes the chase camera lean into corners (Subtle or Strong).
  - **FINISH CAMERA: Set pieces** cycles through camera angles at the finish line, from the game's own data.
  - **CRASH CAMERA: Off** keeps the view behind your car when you crash, instead of cutting to the crash camera.
- **Reflections and shadows** (the picture only):
  - **REFLECTION DISTANCE** (Game, 200 m or 300 m), **REFLECTION DETAIL** (the track, the roadside props, the other
    cars), **REFLECTION RESOLUTION** and **REFLECTION UPDATE**. The ULTRA preset turns them up; the others keep the
    game's reflections.
  - **PROP SHADOWS** (with SUN SHADOWS): lamp posts, signs, barriers and gantries cast shadows on the road.
- **TUNNEL LIGHTS** (GRAPHICS > LIGHTING): the light fittings of tunnels and underpasses glow by day (**Glow**, the
  default), or as on the Xbox, through the bloom (**Xbox**). **Game** keeps the PS2's look.
- **Direct3D 12 on Windows.** GRAPHICS API: Auto (the default) draws with Vulkan, and with Direct3D 12 when no working
  Vulkan device can be created. Pick either if the picture shows faults with your graphics card.
- **More HD texture packs.** Packs with PNG and BC7 files load, and so do files with any letters in their names. HD
  TEXTURE MEMORY (Auto, Full quality or Compressed) keeps a large pack within your graphics card's memory.
- **HD texture cache.** The textures a pack needs converting are converted once and kept on disk (up to 2 GB, only
  what you use), so tracks load faster from then on. The launcher's HD Textures page shows the cache, with **Clear
  cache**.
- **SOUND OUTPUT** (AUDIO > OUTPUT): **Clean** (the default) evens out the loudest moments instead of clipping them,
  and the sound no longer crackles when the PC is busy. **Original** keeps the PS2's own output.
- **Your own music at an even volume.** CUSTOM MUSIC LOUDNESS: Even out (the default) plays songs from different
  sources at the same loudness. Each song is measured once.
- **Car stats and turn signals.** The car select shows GRIP and ACCELERATION too (CAR STATS; **Game** keeps the
  original two). TURN SIGNALS (off by default): the other racers' indicators blink before a bend.
- **HUD and sound extras** (all off by default):
  - **RPM METER** and **BOOST BAR SHAKE** (DISPLAY > RACE HUD).
  - **TAKEDOWN STREAK PITCH** (AUDIO > RACE SOUNDS): each takedown after your 10th since your last crash sounds
    higher.
  - **DRIVER LABELS** (GAME > INTERFACE): the rivals' names, the distance to them, or both, over their cars.
- **START AT** (GAME > STARTING AND LOADING): skip the intro films and start at the title, or go straight to the main
  menu with your profile loaded.
- **Trigger rumble on Windows.** TRIGGER RUMBLE now reaches the trigger motors of Xbox One and Series controllers on
  Windows. It applies at the next start. (Issue #16.)
- **Car mods from older builds** (files saved in the 0x15 / 0x16 format) load like any other.

## Changed

- **Less work for the processor.** The game's own code does about a third less work in a race (its vector maths), so
  it keeps up better on older and slower processors. The game plays exactly the same. (Issue #15, improved.)
- **Races start smoothly.** The cars of a race are loaded while its loading screen is up, and the race waits until
  its track and cars are ready, so its first seconds no longer hitch, with or without an HD pack.
- **The rain's sound** is quieter and follows the game's own sound: it stops in the menus, the pause menu and photo
  mode, and RAIN VOLUME changes it at once. Night frames also take less work to draw.

- **Next-start settings ask what to do.** Leaving SETTINGS in the game with a "next start" row changed asks whether to
  **Keep** it for the next start, **Apply and restart** (the game starts again at the title screen), or **Revert**.
- **SETTINGS BACK BUTTON.** On the game's own settings pages (DRIVER DETAILS > SETTINGS), Back now keeps your changes,
  as confirm does. **Cancel** gives the PS2's behaviour, where Back puts the old values back.
- **FRAME RATE LIMIT: Auto** is the new default: 60 on a processor with 4 threads or fewer, so the game itself keeps
  up, and no limit on others. When the game falls behind, the frame rate is held to 60 until it catches up. If you
  had kept the old default (Unlimited), you now get Auto; a limit you chose is kept. (Issue #15.)
- **CAR SERIES ON L1 / R1** is on by default, including for existing settings: the shoulder buttons change the series
  on SELECT CAR.
- **PRESS START at once.** The title takes Start as soon as it shows, instead of a second later.
- **FRAMES IN FLIGHT: Auto settles.** It no longer switches back and forth every few seconds when the graphics card is
  close to the limit, and a switch no longer stutters.
- The port's own menu rows (SETTINGS, QUIT GAME, PHOTO MODE) and the main menu's SETTINGS help are in the game's
  language.
- **The car pictures on SELECT CAR** are drawn from each car's 3D model, in your paint.
- Bug reports say more: a crash of the port's own code names where it happened, and the log notes how evenly the race
  moved and each real sound dropout.

## Fixed

- **Smoother motion.** With frame interpolation on, a race could stutter slightly all the way through, even with clean
  frame times. The picture now follows the game's own steady 60 Hz timing.
- **Stutter in the menus.** World Tour's event pages could stall the menus and their sound for seconds while an event's
  preview film started. Together with the smoother motion above, this fixes the picture and sound stutter reported
  with 1.3.0. (Issue #17.)
- **Races in slow motion on older processors** (4 threads or fewer) are better: see FRAME RATE LIMIT and the lighter
  load on the processor above. A very slow processor may still not reach full speed. (Issue #15.)
- **RECORD REPLAYS** now records each session started from the launcher. The setting was read before the game had
  loaded your settings, so it stayed off.
- **A crash in heavy traffic.** Wrecking a lot of traffic in a long crash (easy with Heavy traffic) could fill the
  game's 64 slots for wrecked vehicles and crash the game. A wreck at rest, far from the race, now makes room.
- A rare crash in a race when a car reached a point where the game read its lane's data wrongly.
- **Trucks carry their cargo.** The flatbed's crates, barrels, boat, hay, logs and cable reels were missing; they are
  on the truck now, and fly off when it crashes.
- **Confirm works over the crash replay.** On CRASH RESULTS your menus' confirm button (A on an Xbox controller) now
  confirms while the crash's replay plays, and it skips the game's films too. (Issue #14.)
- The Asia and Europe taxis were drawn as the US cab.
- Wrecked traffic hit by other traffic rolls as it does on the PS2 again.
- **The Crash Nav globe** (World Tour's SELECT REGION) was missing at any RENDER SCALE other than 100%.
- **HD texture packs:** a pack file with broken smaller versions of a texture (mip levels) drew it black from a
  distance (in a tunnel, a dark band on the walls). Those versions are now made from the full-size picture.
- ROAD SHINE no longer whitens the sky.
- The track pages no longer show a "Time of day" label for an option that was not in the game yet.
- The launcher's check of an imported DJ voice no longer fails when the launcher has a game folder set.
- Windows: starting the game with `--log` no longer hangs.

## Known limits

- **Night and rain are experimental:** still being finished.
- **The Signature Takedown's flash** is still being looked into. (Issue #17.)
- **Races with 6 or 7 rivals:** the 6th and 7th rival drive the same cars as two of the others, in another paint, and
  start behind the grid. Single Event Race only.
- **DRIVER LABELS:** on the US disc the rivals are named DRIVER1 to DRIVER5, as the game names them.
- **Manual gears:** first and reverse still come by themselves from neutral, and the game picks the gear when your car
  is put back on the road.
- **Windows, TRIGGER RUMBLE:** it changes how the game reads controllers. If a controller behaves differently with it
  on, turn it off and tell us.
- Replays from 1.3.0 and earlier play as before. A replay made with custom cars plays back only with the same cars in
  your CUSTOM folder.
