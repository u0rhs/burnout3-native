# Burnout 3 native 1.2.1 (release notes)

## New

- **Add to Steam.** The launcher's Settings page has a **Steam** section: **Add to Steam** puts the game in the Steam
  library of every Steam account on the computer, with its own cover, banner, background, logo and icon, so it looks
  right in Big Picture and on the Steam Deck. Choose your own pictures with **Cover image...**, **Background image...**
  and **Logo image...**; **Find art online...** opens SteamGridDB's art for the game in your browser. Close Steam first
  (on a Steam Deck: Desktop Mode). Starting the game from Steam also stops Steam's desktop controller layout from
  clicking on the desktop while you play.

- **Custom music volume.** CUSTOM MUSIC VOLUME (PC SETTINGS > INTERFACE > MUSIC, and the launcher's Music page) turns
  your own songs up or down, 0-200%, on top of RADIO VOLUME: many music files are mastered quieter than the game's own
  songs. A limiter keeps loud songs at 200% from distorting.

## Changed

- **Each controller gets its own console's layout.** An Xbox, Switch or other controller now plays as the original Xbox
  game (the triggers the pedals, A boost, B Crashbreaker, X look back, Y camera; A confirms and B goes back in the
  menus), and a PlayStation controller as the PS2 game with the triggers as the pedals (Cross / R2 accelerate, Square /
  L2 brake, R1 boost, the Crashbreaker on R3 and the music on L3, and the right stick as the analog throttle).
  **CONTROLLER LAYOUT** (PC SETTINGS > INTERFACE > CONTROLS, and the launcher's Controllers page) chooses it per
  player: **Auto** (the default: by the controller connected, again when it changes), **Xbox**, **PlayStation** or
  **Custom**, and shows which one is in use. Controller bindings you changed before stay exactly as they were (Custom);
  editing any controller binding switches to Custom. Controller bindings can now differ while driving and in the menus,
  like the keyboard's.

- **Update check at every start.** The launcher now asks whether a newer release is out each time it starts (it used to
  be once a day), so a new release shows up straight away.

- **The CONTROLS page in your language.** Every row is now translated: the players' driving and menu controls and every
  shortcut (screenshot, bug report, photo mode and the rest), in the game and in the launcher.

## Fixed

- DRIVER DETAILS > RECORDS: the car icons sit beside their names again (they were drawn about two rows low, at every
  screen shape).
- **Smoother on a busy Windows PC.** The game now runs at a higher priority than ordinary programs, so a download, a
  build or another emulator in the background no longer causes short freezes in races.
- The **Crashbreaker** button prompt on **R3** now shows a stick button, not a shoulder button.
- At ultrawide aspects (21:9, 32:9) the race results page's stat labels (BEST LAP, TAKEDOWNS, ...) slid to the
  screen's left edge, away from their values; they now stay on their bars.
- With CAMERA DISTANCE or CAMERA HEIGHT changed, the takedown camera (a Signature Takedown's too) and the crash cameras
  were pulled back and raised as well, so they no longer framed the crash as the game does. Only the race camera moves
  now.
- The French PC SETTINGS title showed a box for its È; letters the game's title font lacks are now drawn plain.
- With an HD texture pack, faint see-through layers of the scenery (the warm sun haze over the buildings in the US
  city, the light pattern in the Europe tunnel) disappeared or turned into blotches; they now look as they do without
  a pack.
- The HD TEXTURES setting's help now says it applies when the game next starts.
