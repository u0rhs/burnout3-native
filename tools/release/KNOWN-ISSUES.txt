Burnout 3 native port -- known issues in this test build (@VERSION@)
(from the developers' open list; report them anyway when you hit them, with a bug report: F10 in the game)

Gameplay / stability
- Very rare crashes or hangs are still possible: a bug report right after one is the most useful thing you can send.

Presentation
- Cars far ahead fade in over the last stretch of the game's draw distance instead of popping in; tell us if any
  still pop in.

Performance
- On slower PCs a race can fall behind real time, and the sound then stutters: lower the settings or try safe mode.

Not in this build
- Online play.
- Mac and Linux builds (Windows only for now).
