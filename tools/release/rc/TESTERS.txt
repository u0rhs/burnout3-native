Burnout 3 native 1.4.0 -- release candidate @VERSION@ (for testers)

Thank you for testing. This is the build we plan to release as 1.4.0, unless you find something that should be
fixed first. It needs your own copy of the US PS2 game (SLUS-21050), as every release does. It uses your existing game
data, saves and settings; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Start it with Burnout3Launcher.exe as usual. When 1.4.0 itself comes out, the launcher offers the update by itself.


WHAT IS NEW IN 1.4.0 (the short version; RELEASE-NOTES.txt has all of it)

- SETTINGS > EXTRAS: everything 1.4.0 adds to the game itself, each with its own On / Off, all off on a new install
  except ELIMINATOR (a fifth mode on SELECT MODE). Race options on SELECT OPTIONS (traffic, laps, manual gears, a
  standing start ...), up to eight cars, more series on SELECT CAR (Heavyweights, every traffic vehicle, your car mods,
  the cut BETA cars from the prototype disc), traffic checking (Revenge / Extreme), party modes, race replays.
  A race with a gameplay extra keeps its results apart from your records.
- Night and rain to try out (EXTRAS > EXPERIMENTAL), also in World Tour.
- Your own colours on SELECT CAR, a camera you can turn in a race, reflections of the world around the car,
  Direct3D 12 on Windows, cleaner sound, smoother motion, and fixes for crashes in heavy traffic.


WHAT TO TRY

1. FIRST: run-all-tests (about 12 minutes, no input needed)
   - Quit the game if it is running. Open the folder run-all-tests and double-click run-all-tests.bat.
   - It plays twelve short scripted sessions in a window, one after another: races by day, at night and in the rain,
     with Vulkan and Direct3D 12, VSYNC, the reflection settings, a race replay with its cameras, an 8-car race and a
     Crash event. Do not press anything; each session ends by itself. Watch the motion: is it smooth?
   - It uses its own copies of your settings and of your save (your own files are not changed), and the game data
     your launcher uses (if it cannot find it, it asks once for the folder or disc image).
   - At the end it shows a table and makes one file: test-results-<date>-<time>.zip, in the run-all-tests folder.
     Please send us that file (see SENDING US THINGS), with a few words on what you saw.
   - (Windows only. On Linux or a Mac, skip this step.)
2. Then play as you like, and try what is new, for example:
   - SETTINGS > EXTRAS: turn on a few extras and play with them: race options, MORE RIVALS (8 cars), TRAFFIC CHECK
     (Revenge or Extreme: you should never crash on a hit the mode allows), MIRROR MODE, PARTY MODES, RACE REPLAY.
   - EXTRAS > EXPERIMENTAL: NIGHT and RAIN, then SELECT OPTIONS' TIME OF DAY and WEATHER rows, and NIGHT / RAIN IN
     WORLD TOUR. Experimental: tell us how it looks and how fast it runs on your PC.
   - If you have them: car mods or a car pack (launcher > Game Data > Custom cars: Import car pack...) and the June
     2004 prototype or demo disc (launcher > Game Data > Beta / Prototype cars), then the CUSTOM and BETA series on
     SELECT CAR.
   - SETTINGS > DISPLAY > FRAME RATE LIMIT is now Auto (60 on a processor with 4 threads or fewer, no limit on
     others). If a race runs slow or stutters on your PC, tell us your processor.
   - An Xbox One / Series controller on Windows: SETTINGS > CONTROLS > FEEDBACK: RUMBLE On and TRIGGER RUMBLE On,
     then restart the game (it asks). Do the triggers rumble? Do they still accelerate and brake properly from the first
     second of a race? With two Xbox controllers too, and on the Xbox Wireless Adapter if you use one. If anything is
     wrong with it on, turn TRIGGER RUMBLE off again and tell us.


SENDING US THINGS

- The run-all-tests zip (step 1).
- A bug report for anything that goes wrong: in the game press F10 ("BUG REPORT SAVED" shows where it went), or in the
  launcher: Play page > Bug report... > Make report. If the game crashed, make the report afterwards: the crashed run
  is in it. You get one file, bug-report-<date-time>.zip, on your Desktop (or in Documents).
- Send the files with a short note on what happened and roughly when (mode, track or menu) to @WHERE@.


WHAT THESE FILES CONTAIN (and what they do not)

- A bug report: the logs and controller recordings of your last 5 runs, your settings files, the port's version, and a
  short description of your system (Windows version, processor, memory, graphics card and driver, display). Your save
  only if you choose "With my save" in the launcher. No game data.
- The run-all-tests zip: for each of its twelve sessions the game's log, its frame timings, the settings it ran with,
  its controller recording and a few screenshots; plus the results table. It does not contain your save (the session
  copies stay on your PC) or any game data.
- In both, your user name is taken out of folder paths (they read ~\... or C:\Users\<user>\...). The logs do name your
  processor and graphics card, and the folders the game and its data are in below your home folder.
- Nothing is sent anywhere by itself: you send the files yourself.
